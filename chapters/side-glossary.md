# Side: Glossary (hypermind)

* **Exocortex inversion:** the point where the external loop is the thinker and the brain is the rater.
* **Comprehension gap:** verified but not understood.
* **Synthetic drift:** self-distribution persisting beyond the self.
* **Preference closure:** agency reduced to like/dislike.
* **Meat-trap:** the affectionate term for the remaining biology.
