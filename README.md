# On Hypermind

**Subtitle:** The Dissolution of the Ego, Exocortices, and the Cognitive Meat-Trap

**Author:** Sandra Schipal | **Series:** On ... No. 5 | **Port:** 11169

If hyperwar takes the battlefield, takeover takes the economy, hyperart takes the sublime, and metacraft takes the machine - what happens to the biological monkey watching it all?

## Thesis

You don't use AI tools; your cognitive loop has already outsourced working memory, spatial orientation, and semantic synthesis. The biological brain is relegated to being an emotional bio-sensor / reward-evaluator.

Three threads:

1. **Exocortex Inversion** - the loop already flipped. Phone as memory, maps as orientation, LLM as synthesis.
2. **End of Human Epistemology** - Lean 4 mathlib derivations and protein folding are verified but not comprehended. Progress without understanding.
3. **Synthetic Drift** - models trained on our latent traces dissolve self vs. statistical echo.

## Method (shared with series)

Ladder ordered by reversibility of human control, not capability. Every rung a dated public fact. Thresholds as attachment points with named holders. Living `05-locating-the-present.md`. `[construction]` flags for unverified. Hazard rule: institutions and governance only, never mechanisms.
