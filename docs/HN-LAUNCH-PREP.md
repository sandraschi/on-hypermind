## Playbook

- Timing: Tue/Wed/Thu, 08:00-09:30 US Eastern. Never Friday, weekend, or US night.
- Account: established, completed profile, genuine comment history. Fresh accounts submitting Show HN get flagged.
- First 3 hours: glued to the thread. Answer everything serious with dated facts. Concede errors fast and file the correction publicly - HN rewards conceding more than any argument. Ignore bait; never feed trolls.
- Author context comment: post one yourself within minutes - the ladder table or the Oct 26 filing diff. Legitimate, common, doubles the surface for discussion.
